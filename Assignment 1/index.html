Part 3: University policies and services

PLAGIARISM

Plagarism is treated seriously. If we suspect any work is copied, all students involved are likely to recieve zero
for the entire assignment. Plagarism has led to students being expelled from University.
Please click here to read more about Plagiarism.

ACADEMIC CONSIDERATION

We know that life isn't perfect and there may be a time when you can't hand in an assignment on time, or make it
to a class or exam. If you find yourself in a situation like this, you may be eligible for Academic Consideration.
Academic Consideration is designed to help students when they are sick, injured or where a serious, unplanned situation has occurred that has affected the ability to study.
Please click here to read more about Academic Consideration.

STUDENT SUPPORT ADVISORS

If you have an issue or a problem that is affecting your study, then the Student Support Advisors may be able to
help. There are Student Support Advisors available to assist students who are studying at all UOW Campuses and
in all UOW Faculities. Please click here to read more about Student Support Advisers.

DISABILITY SERVICES

Disability Service provides advice and support for students with disability or health condition. Our aim is to
ensure students with disability realise their full academic potential. We also provide support for those who are
primary carers for those with a medical condition or disability.
Please click here to read more about Disability Services.
